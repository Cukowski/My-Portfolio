About me

I work on engineering software at enGits in Germany, supporting software development, testing and Windows/Linux compatibility, including work with DrNUM and DucsiFlow. I also contribute to the publicly released enGits Data Library and open-source tooling.

I enjoy getting unfamiliar systems to work: understanding the requirements, investigating the problem and checking the result. In my own projects, I also use Codex and Claude as part of my development workflow.

I have completed the academic requirements for Software Engineering at Amsterdam Tech, with diploma issuance pending. I am also studying International Business Information Systems at Furtwangen University.

My next step: a company-based bachelor’s thesis, ideally with the opportunity to stay on afterwards. I am particularly interested in engineering software, cybersecurity and aerospace, and open to relocating within Germany.
